GOOD QUESTIONS

Quick answers.
Clear next steps.

Who can use StudyBuddy?

University, high-school and junior-secondary learners, as well as anyone studying independently.

Can I install it on my phone?

Yes. Use the Install button in a supported browser. On iPhone, open StudyBuddy in Safari and choose Share, then Add to Home Screen.

How does Premium work?

Premium costs KSh 50 for seven days. You complete checkout through Paystack and renew manually when your access expires.

Does StudyBuddy replace teachers?

No. It is a learning companion designed to explain, guide and help you practise. Always follow your teacher's instructions and verify important information.
