YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy Policy

Draft policy — review and obtain appropriate legal advice before public launch.

StudyBuddy collects information you submit when registering, such as your name, email address, phone number and learning level. It may process questions and messages you send to the AI tutor to provide educational responses.

How information is used

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To provide AI study assistance and maintain service security.
  • To verify Premium payments and manage access.
  • To respond to support requests and improve the service.

Service providers

StudyBuddy may use Supabase for authentication and database services, OpenAI to generate AI responses, Netlify to host the website and Paystack to process payments. These providers process data as needed to provide their services under their own terms and policies.

Children and younger learners

StudyBuddy is intended for a broad student audience, including younger learners. Before public launch, the operator must set an age policy, implement appropriate parental/guardian consent where required, minimise personal data collection and provide a safe reporting process. Younger users should not share sensitive personal information in AI chats.

Security and retention

Reasonable technical safeguards should be used. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Account and payment records may be retained as needed for service operation, legal compliance and dispute resolution.
